Root causes (in nested IT fixture build config)

   - Maven defaults to platform encoding (ANSI_X3.4-1968 / ASCII in this environment).

2) Test compilation fails: "cannot find symbol: class MoreObjects" (com.google.common.base.MoreObjects).
   - The fixture compiles against Guava 17.0 (see build.log downloading guava-17.0.jar).
   - MoreObjects is not present in Guava 17.
   - The fixture declares guava-testlib 18.0 but Maven resolves guava 17.0 via transitive deps (e.g., auto-value or other deps), so we need to force a newer guava.

  b) Configure maven-compiler-plugin encoding to UTF-8.
