Root cause:
- The submodules (common, factory, service, value) declare:
    <parent>
      <groupId>com.google.auto</groupId>
      <artifactId>auto-parent</artifactId>
      <version>3</version>
    </parent>
  This pulls the published parent POM com.google.auto:auto-parent:3 from Maven Central.
- auto-parent:3 declares a system-scoped dependency on com.sun:tools with systemPath
  pointing at ${java.home}/../lib/tools.jar (JDK 8 layout).
- On JDK 9+, tools.jar no longer exists, so Maven fails dependency resolution before
  compilation.

Secondary issue likely after fixing the above:
- The repo root parent POM (./pom.xml, auto-parent 4-SNAPSHOT) sets <java.version>1.6</java.version>.
  JDK 11 does not support compiling with -source/-target 1.6.

   This avoids the external auto-parent:3 and removes the tools.jar dependency.
