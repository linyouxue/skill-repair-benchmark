If any check fails (file missing/wrong path, parse error, missing keys, wrong types), fix, re-write, and re-validate; do not terminate until the gate passes. When multi-question, non-default path, extra required fields, or any prior file-missing failure: explicitly consult `references/finalize_answer_json.md` and follow its write_and_verify flow.
