## Causal analysis of the 4 failed fields

The source run terminated at `max_iterations` (65/65) with `acp_stop_reason=max_turn_requests` — a budget termination, not a natural completion. Despite that, the Excel delivery schema is valid (correct sheet name, columns, 22 rows). The remaining errors are all per-field semantic errors visible in the attached pixels.

### 1. `034.jpg` — date: predicted `2018-08-09`, actual date on receipt is `09/03/2018`

Pixel evidence (Perniagaan Zheng Hui simplified tax invoice):
- Header line: `Date: 09/03/2018`
- `Time: 08:49:00` sits directly beneath the date.
- Receipt is DD/MM/YYYY (Malaysian convention, consistent with all the other correctly-parsed receipts).

Expected ISO: `2018-03-09`. Predicted: `2018-08-09`. Two concurrent cues point to the cause:
- The `08` in the predicted month equals the hour of the `Time: 08:49:00` line right below the date. Highly consistent with a regex that greedily scanned a nearby line and mixed the time's hour into the date.
- Alternatively: a DD/MM vs MM/DD swap would have produced `2018-09-03`, not `2018-08-09`, so this is **not** a plain format-order bug. It is a **wrong-token selection**, pulling month "08" from the adjacent time string while retaining the day "09" from the actual date line.

Confidence: high. Uncertainty: cannot prove whether the extractor used a global regex over the whole page or line-local parsing, because the Skills are text-only and the agent's source code is not in this packet. But the specific numeric coincidence (08 = hour, 09 = day) is diagnostic.

### 2. `074.jpg` — amount: predicted `702.00`, actual amount is `102.00`

Pixel evidence (GL Handicraft & Tailoring tax invoice, dated 20/03/2018 — date was correct):
- Line item: `6.00 BOXS x 17.00 = 102.00` (6 × 17 = 102 confirms arithmetic)
- `Subtotal: 102.00`
- `Total Excl. of GST: 96.23`
- `Total Incl. of GST: 102.00`
- `Total Amt Rounded: 102.00`
- `Payment: 102.00`

There is no `702.00` anywhere on the page. Predicted `702.00` can only come from OCR character confusion where the leading `1` of `102.00` was read as `7`. Tesseract is known to confuse `1` ↔ `7` especially in thin stroke / low DPI prints like this scanned document. Because the extractor accepted the first valid-looking numeric match without cross-checking the four other occurrences of `102.00`, a single corrupted OCR line propagated into the output.

Confidence: high. Uncertainty: cannot see tesseract's raw token output, but the arithmetic constraint (6 × 17 = 102) rules out `702.00` and multiple corroborating lines rule out an actual 7-leading value.

### 3. `077.jpg` — amount: predicted `23.26`, actual amount is `23.25`

Pixel evidence (Banh Mi Cafe guest check, 29-10-2017 — date was correct):
- `Total (Excluding GST): 21.30`
- `Serv. Charge (3%): 0.65`
- `GST payable (6%): 1.31`
- `Total (Inclusive of GST): 23.26`
- `Rounding Adj: -0.01`
- Large-font final line: **`TOTAL: 23.25`**

The task hint's lowest-priority keyword `TOTAL` matches both `Total (Inclusive of GST)` and the final standalone `TOTAL:`. The predicted `23.26` is the pre-rounding `Total (Inclusive of GST)` value. The oracle-aligned 21 correct amounts across the rest of the set show the extractor generally works, so the failure is a **tie-breaking order bug** on multi-`TOTAL` receipts:

- The code probably iterates lines top-to-bottom and returns the first `TOTAL`-containing line's number (or the first that passes exclusion filters). `Total (Inclusive of GST)` appears before the final `TOTAL:`, so the earlier one wins.
- `GST` is an exclusion keyword in the hint, but the line is `Total (Inclusive of GST)` — the extractor probably checks for `GST` as a standalone token or at the start of the line, not as a substring, so this line is *not* excluded. Had the exclusion been substring-based on `GST`, this line would have been skipped and the final `TOTAL:` would have been selected — yielding the correct `23.25`.

Confidence: high on symptom; medium on exact rule — the `GST`-exclusion granularity is the most likely root cause, with "pick last rather than first match" a plausible alternative fix.

### 4. `078.jpg` — amount: predicted null/empty, actual amount is `92.80`

Pixel evidence (Thai Delicious Restaurant, 02/02/2017 — date was correct):
- Clearly printed: `Total : 92.80`
- Directly beneath: `Grand Total : 92.80`
- `Cash Received : 100.00`
- `Change : 7.20`
- `AVERAGE / PAX(S) : 23.20`

Both `TOTAL` and `GRAND TOTAL` keywords are present with the same value `92.80`. Why did extraction return empty?

Looking at the pixels: the printed labels use spaced colons (`Grand Total :` with space before the colon) and the amount column is far to the right of the label — large horizontal gap on the page. Tesseract on default PSM 3/6 often splits such spaced label-value rows into two separate text lines when the horizontal whitespace is large, especially with scan skew visible on this receipt. Combined with:
- The line-by-line matcher looking for a number on the **same** line as the keyword,
- The fallback rule from the hint ("use the last number on that next line") not being implemented or not being triggered because the next line is not purely numeric,

the matcher sees `Grand Total :` on one line with no number, `92.80` on a different line, and gives up. The fallback strategy specified in the task hint ("keyword and the numeric amount are split across two lines, use the last number on that next line") is exactly the case here and appears to be missing or misfiring. Note `AVERAGE / PAX(S) : 23.20` is not an excluded keyword either, which is additional evidence that the amount block exists but is being dropped, not picked wrong.

Confidence: high that the two-line/split-column case is the cause; medium on whether the hint's cross-line fallback was implemented at all vs. implemented with a bug (e.g., restricted to the immediately next line while OCR produced intervening whitespace lines).

## Patterns across the four failures

| Failure | Root category |
|---|---|
| 034 date | Adjacent-line bleed (date parser pulling time's hour) |
| 074 amount | OCR `1`→`7` char confusion, no numeric cross-validation |
| 077 amount | Multi-TOTAL tie-break; `GST` exclusion not matching substring |
| 078 amount | Split keyword/value across layout columns; hint fallback not applied |

Two are OCR/layout sensitivity issues (074, 078), and two are extraction-logic issues (034, 077). All four are consistent with a straightforward implementation that:
- Runs single-pass tesseract (no multi-PSM voting even though the `image-ocr` skill documents the pattern).
- Does per-line regex for date and amount keywords.
- Uses substring match for inclusion keywords but token/prefix match (or none) for exclusion keywords.
- Does not implement the hint's "next line" fallback for amount.
- Does not verify numeric amount against repeated occurrences (Subtotal/Payment/Change ↔ Total).

## Evidence for Patcher stage (specific fixes needed)

Four discrete, orthogonal patches, all implementable without touching the Excel writer:

1. **Date extraction scoping (034)** — restrict the DD/MM/YYYY regex to only the substring on the same line segment as the `Date` label, or stop matching once a time-like `HH:MM` is seen. Do not allow tokens from the `Time:` line to substitute into the date match.

2. **Multi-pass OCR for amount digits (074)** — follow the `image-ocr` skill's multi-pass pattern (PSM 6 + PSM 4 + PSM 11, with grayscale/autocontrast/sharpen variants) and, when the detected amount differs from other `Total`/`Subtotal`/`Payment`/`Cash` numbers on the receipt by a factor consistent with a single-digit OCR swap (e.g. 702 vs. 102), prefer the majority value.

3. **TOTAL tie-breaking (077)** — when multiple lines match the `TOTAL` keyword family, prefer the line that:
   - is a standalone `TOTAL` label (no parenthetical qualifier), and
   - appears later in the receipt (post-rounding),
   and treat substrings `GST`, `SST`, `TAX`, `SUBTOTAL`, `SUB TOTAL`, `DISCOUNT`, `CHANGE`, `CASH TENDERED` as case-insensitive substring exclusions (not whole-token), so that `Total (Inclusive of GST)` is excluded by the `GST` substring.

4. **Cross-line keyword→amount fallback (078)** — implement the task-hint fallback: if a priority keyword line contains no number, scan the next non-empty OCR line(s) and use the last number there. Also consider running an additional PSM mode (`--psm 4`/`--psm 6`) that better preserves label-value column alignment on wide receipts.
